The Climate Change Act Support Platform (CCASP) provides practical guidance, tools, training and resources to help municipalities, districts and practitioners strengthen climate change mitigation planning, meet legislative requirements and build lasting institutional capacity. What the CCASP covers

The Climate Change Act Support Platform (CCASP) has been developed to assist municipalities, districts, practitioners and other stakeholders in implementing the requirements of South Africa's Climate Change Act.
The platform provides practical guidance, training materials, tools and resources to strengthen climate change mitigation planning, support legislative compliance, and build institutional capacity for effective climate action.

Climate change planning encompasses both adaptation and mitigation.
Adaptation focuses on reducing vulnerability and building resilience to the impacts of climate change that are already occurring or are expected in the future.
Mitigation focuses on reducing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and enhancing carbon sinks to limit the extent of future climate change.
While South Africa has made significant progress in supporting adaptation planning through initiatives such as the Let's Respond Toolkit (link to Toolkit website), there remains a need for practical guidance to support climate change mitigation planning and implementation. CCASP has therefore been developed to address this gap.

The platform provides practical guidance and resources to support:

  • Climate Change Mitigation Needs and Response Assessments (CCAMNRAs)
  • Climate Change Mitigation Response Implementation Plans (CCAMRIPs)
  • Greenhouse gas (GHG) inventory development
  • Stakeholder engagement and GEDSI considerations
  • Climate change mitigation planning and implementation
Together, these modules provide a structured, step-by-step approach to implementing the mitigation requirements of the Climate Change Act at the sub-national level.
