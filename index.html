CCASP is organised around four routes through the same body of guidance. Pick the one that matches the work in front of you.

For municipalities & districts Understand the Climate Change Act & Planning Framework Get oriented with the big picture: what the Climate Change Act requires of districts and municipalities, and how CCASP provides a structured, step-by-step approach to implementation at sub-national level. For anyone wanting a high-level understanding of how the Act works and how CCASP fits South Africa's climate action strategy. For practitioners Leverage Our GHG Inventory & Assessment Tools Get hands-on with the practical instruments for tracking emissions and conducting needs assessments — templates, methodologies and workbooks you can use directly. For those responsible for data collection, analysis and reporting related to greenhouse gas inventories. For stakeholders Develop & Implement Your Mitigation Response Plan Work through the core process of creating an effective mitigation plan — practical guidance for producing CCAMRIPs, engaging stakeholders and embedding GEDSI considerations. For those developing and executing mitigation strategies, from local government officials to NGOs working on community-level action. For learners & facilitators Explore Our Training Modules & Resources Go deeper with targeted training. CCASP offers structured modules, facilitator materials, case studies and a supporting resource library. For individuals or groups needing in-depth knowledge on specific aspects of mitigation planning and implementation.

What CCASP covers

South Africa has made strong progress on adaptation planning through initiatives such as the Let's Respond Toolkit. CCASP addresses the remaining gap: practical support for mitigation planning and implementation at sub-national level.

  • Climate Change Mitigation Needs and Response Assessments (CCAMNRAs)
  • Climate Change Mitigation Response Implementation Plans (CCAMRIPs)
  • Greenhouse gas (GHG) inventory development
  • Stakeholder engagement and GEDSI considerations
  • Climate change mitigation planning and implementation
