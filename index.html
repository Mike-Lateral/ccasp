Climate Change Act Support Platform for Mitigation in South Africa

The Climate Change Act Support Platform (CCASP) provides practical guidance, training materials, tools and resources to help municipalities, districts, practitioners and other stakeholders implement South Africa's Climate Change Act. Climate change planning covers both adaptation and mitigation. The CCASP addresses the remaining gap in mitigation support.

CCASP is organised around four categories of information.

Climate Change Act Get oriented with the big picture: what the Climate Change Act requires of districts and municipalities, and how CCASP provides a structured, step-by-step approach to implementation at sub-national level.
